Aaryan Saroha

X Profile LinkedIn

Aaryan Saroha is the Founder and Chief Executive Officer of Quantify Terminal, the financial intelligence infrastructure that compiles what changes in the world into an investor's models, theses, portfolio risk and decisions.

His work sits at the intersection of quantitative finance and financial engineering: pricing and valuation frameworks, factor and risk models, market microstructure, and the data infrastructure required to run them at production scale. That focus defines how Quantify is designed, around the decision workflow of a professional desk rather than around isolated charts and screens.

He founded Quantify Terminal on a simple observation: terminals tell investors what happened, then leave the consequences to spreadsheets, notes and memory. Quantify carries those consequences automatically. A filing enters the Data Fabric with a canonical identifier and point-in-time timestamps, the Knowledge Compiler extracts its claims, the Financial Model Compiler proposes the cells it changes, the thesis graph re-scores the assumptions it touches, the Portfolio Digital Twin recomputes the exposure it moves, and the Decision Ledger records what was done about it and why.

His engineering background shapes the technical direction: a canonical data layer with machine-readable rights, a deterministic finance engine, event-driven model and thesis updates, portfolio look-through risk, and a native desktop terminal that stays responsive under institutional workloads. He leads architecture and product strategy directly, from ingestion through to the analytics surfaced on the desk.

The terminal covers live markets, research, portfolios, risk, derivatives, funds, macro and geopolitics and serves quantitative analysts, hedge funds, portfolio managers, risk and compliance teams, family offices and institutional investors. He builds it on the view that professional infrastructure should be fast, transparent and quantitatively rigorous, and that its economics should not restrict access to firms of a certain size.

He is building Quantify Terminal as long-term financial infrastructure: the system that maintains conviction, not just information.
