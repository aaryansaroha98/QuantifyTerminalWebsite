Aaryan Saroha

Founder and Chief Executive Officer of Quantify Terminal, working across quantitative finance, market microstructure, and financial technology. Electrical Engineering, Indian Institute of Technology Jammu.

X Profile LinkedIn

Aaryan Saroha is the Founder and Chief Executive Officer of Quantify Terminal, an institutional-grade market intelligence platform used for research, portfolio construction, risk management, and systematic execution.

His work sits at the intersection of quantitative finance and financial engineering: pricing and valuation frameworks, factor and risk models, market microstructure, and the data infrastructure required to run them at production scale. That focus defines how the terminal is designed — around the decision workflow of a professional desk rather than around isolated charts and screens.

He founded Quantify Terminal to consolidate infrastructure that most institutions assemble from separate, expensive, and poorly integrated systems. The platform delivers 35+ workspace tabs, 450+ analytical panels, and coverage across 500M+ instruments in one desktop environment, serving quantitative analysts, hedge funds, portfolio managers, risk and compliance teams, algorithmic traders, and institutional investors.

His engineering background shapes the technical direction of the product: real-time streaming market data, low-latency analytics, systematic backtesting, and execution tooling engineered for reliability under load. He leads architecture and product strategy directly, from data ingestion through to the analytics surfaced on the desk.

He is building Quantify Terminal on the view that institutional market infrastructure should be fast, transparent, and quantitatively rigorous, and that the economics of professional-grade tooling should not restrict access to firms of a certain size.

He is building Quantify Terminal as long-term financial infrastructure: a platform engineered to institutional standards of accuracy, performance, and operational discipline.
