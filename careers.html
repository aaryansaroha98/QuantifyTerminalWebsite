Benefits

Why intern at Quantify Terminal?

Real ownership, real product, real impact, the kind of experience that compounds.

Real-world experience

Work on a live, institutional-grade platform used by quantitative analysts, hedge funds, portfolio managers, and risk teams, not throwaway practice projects.

Direct mentorship

Work side by side with the founder and learn how quantitative finance, data, and engineering come together in practice.

Visible impact

Ship features that reach real users. Your work lands in the product, not in a drawer, and you see it in action.

Certificate & reference

Earn a completion certificate and a genuine reference letter that reflects the work you actually shipped.

Ownership from day one

Take real responsibility for your work. You own features end to end, from idea and design through to release.

Flexible & remote

Fully remote with flexible hours. Work around your studies while still making meaningful progress on the product.

Learn fast

Move quickly through real problems in markets, data, and product. You will grow more in months than in most year-long roles.

Path to full-time

Strong interns are first in line as the team grows. Prove yourself and there is a clear route to a long-term role.
