Feature inventory

Every major workflow has a working surface.

Quantify Terminal is a 22+ workspace desktop terminal covering market monitoring, research, portfolios, watchlists, screeners, alerts, workspace tools, relationship maps, strategy development, news, economics, and account operations.

Market cockpit

Dashboard, market data, heatmap, news, live monitors.

Monitor index snapshots, world regions, US markets, European markets, Asian markets, commodities, currencies, bonds, rates, sectors, futures, volatility, crypto, ETF flow, movers, calendars, headlines, trending topics, saved articles, and live market panes.

Research suite

Analysis, equity research, filings, products, peers.

Study a symbol through financial summaries, key ratios, valuation, growth, technical analysis, comparison views, DCF work, analyst context, ownership, ESG context, recent news, earnings, options, filings, historical data, product analytics, and peer tables.

Portfolio and lists

Portfolio, watchlist, screener, alerts.

Track holdings, order preparation, P&L, allocation, performance analytics, risk metrics, transaction history, sector exposure, tax lots, dividend calendars, correlations, stress scenarios, saved watchlists, symbol detail, chart previews, technical scorecards, filters, alert groups, and alert history.

Strategy work

Algo trading, Quant Studio, trade desk.

Create rules, test strategies, review equity curves, inspect signal logs, study correlations, track positions, manage order context, use risk lab views, review trade logs, and prepare execution decisions inside the same terminal environment.

Intelligence layer

Economics, geo intelligence, geopolitics, supply chain, relationship map.

Move beyond price action with macro context, geographic layers, geopolitical monitoring, relationship graphs, supplier and customer mapping, revenue context, company connections, and broader market narratives.

Workspace system

Notes, journals, spreadsheet workflows, exports, account controls.

Use notebooks, workbook surfaces, formula guides, connected sheets, export schedules, formula builder, journal entries, trade logs, profile controls, settings, update metadata, and account-level operations.

Panel ergonomics

Dense screens without losing control.

The platform uses panels that can be rearranged, resized, detached, duplicated, collapsed, exported, linked by symbol, and reset when a workflow needs a clean start.

Move
