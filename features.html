Features

An institutional market desk built around real workflows.

35+ workspace tabs, 450+ panels, 113 searchable functions, and coverage across 500M+ instruments. Quantify Terminal brings research, live market views, portfolios, risk management, macro context, broker connectivity, quantitative strategy, algorithmic execution, options, bonds, derivatives, mutual funds, dividends, and insider activity into a single desktop environment built for quantitative analysts, hedge funds, portfolio managers, risk teams, and institutional investors.

Core modules · 35+ tabs · 450+ panels · 113 functions

Workspaces that belong together.

Every module is designed to support the next step: discover a move, understand it, compare it, track it, hedge it, and act with context.

Market command center

The command center keeps the entire market in front of you at once. Live index tables span every major region — the US, Europe, Asia, and beyond — alongside commodities, currencies, and crypto, all laid out for fast scanning and side-by-side comparison. Watchlists update in real time, so the names you follow never leave your sight, and the layout is tuned for the way professionals actually open the day: one sweep of global conditions before drilling into anything specific. It is built for heavy daily use, not a decorative dashboard.

Watchlists Index tables Commodities & forex Crypto

Equity research workspace

Research lives in one continuous workspace instead of a dozen disconnected tabs. Company overview, full financial statements, valuation models, key ratios, and live news sit together with integrated chart work, so a single name can be analysed from end to end without ever switching tools. Move from the headline numbers down to the underlying statements, check the story against the latest news, and frame a valuation — all in the same view. The result is research that holds onto its context, so your thinking carries forward instead of starting over.

Company summary Financials Valuation & DCF News context

Portfolio intelligence

Portfolio views turn raw positions into a clear picture of what is actually happening. Holdings, allocation, performance over time, transaction history, and a live position heatmap are combined so you can see what changed, why it matters, and where your attention should go next. Track contribution and drawdown, spot concentration before it becomes a problem, and review every transaction in context. The focus is deliberately practical: less admin, and a far sharper sense of how the book is really behaving.

Holdings Performance Transactions Position heatmap

Geopolitics & global risk

Markets never move in isolation, so the terminal keeps world events on the same screen as your positions. A live incident map tracks conflicts, disasters, and political events by category, severity, and location, while country intelligence panels add the context behind each headline. Follow escalation as it happens, watch air traffic and regional activity, and understand how a shock in one region ripples into sectors and prices — the difference between reacting to a number and understanding what is actually driving it.

Incident map Country intelligence Severity tracking Global risk

Ownership & insider activity

See who really owns a company and how that is changing. The ownership tab ranks top institutional holders with share counts and ownership percentages, tracks recent insider transactions by officers and directors, and visualizes holder concentration so you can spot how much of the float sits in a few hands. Free-float, strategic holdings, and insider activity are combined in one view, so you can judge conviction, crowding, and control before you act on a name.

Top holders Insider transactions Holder concentration Free float

Screeners, heatmaps & strategy

This is where broad market structure becomes a focused action list. Screeners filter the entire market down to the names that match your criteria, heatmaps reveal sector rotation and momentum at a glance, and the quant studio lets you build, backtest, and refine strategy logic. Move from a high-level scan to specific, tested ideas without ever leaving the environment. Filtering, pattern recognition, and strategy work sit side by side, so research flows naturally into a concrete plan you can act on.

Screeners Heatmaps Backtesting Strategy logic

Options chain, greeks & flow

The options workspace turns a chain into a full derivatives surface. Inspect bid, ask, last price, implied volatility, volume, open interest, delta, gamma, theta, vega, and rho by strike, then move into IV smile, delta-by-strike, gamma exposure, volume, open interest, and max-pain views. Flow positioning summarizes put/call ratios, median IV, total contracts, and strike concentration so traders can read pressure around the underlying instead of staring at a raw chain in isolation.

Options chain Greeks IV smile Max pain

Funds, bonds & income analytics

Portfolio construction extends beyond single stocks. Mutual fund screens track live NAV data, fund detail, TER, NAV performance, drawdown, daily returns, top performers, SIP projections, and goal-planning paths. The bond desk adds government yield curves and tenor history, while dividend tools capture upcoming payouts, ex-dividend dates, published records, annual dividend totals, growth rates, yield screening, and income planning.

Mutual funds SIP goals Yield curves Dividends

Risk, disclosures & market microstructure

Risk is tracked at both portfolio and security level. Beta security risk compares assets against benchmarks with indexed performance, daily return history, historical and parametric VaR, correlation, Sharpe ratio, and drawdown. Disclosure desks track insider buy/sell activity, director transactions, bulk deals, block deals, promoter holding changes, pledge data, and derivatives participant positioning, making ownership, liquidity, and institutional flow easier to connect with price action.
