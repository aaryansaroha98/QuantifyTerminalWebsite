Features

A market desk built around real workflows.

Quantify Terminal brings together research, live market views, portfolios, macro context, broker support, strategy work, and alerts in a single desktop environment that feels fast, focused, and professional.

Core modules

Workspaces that belong together.

Every module is designed to support the next step: discover a move, understand it, compare it, track it, and act with context.

Market command center

The command center keeps the entire market in front of you at once. Live index tables span every major region — the US, Europe, Asia, and beyond — alongside commodities, currencies, and crypto, all laid out for fast scanning and side-by-side comparison. Watchlists update in real time, so the names you follow never leave your sight, and the layout is tuned for the way professionals actually open the day: one sweep of global conditions before drilling into anything specific. It is built for heavy daily use, not a decorative dashboard.

Watchlists Index tables Commodities & forex Crypto

Equity research workspace

Research lives in one continuous workspace instead of a dozen disconnected tabs. Company overview, full financial statements, valuation models, key ratios, and live news sit together with integrated chart work, so a single name can be analysed from end to end without ever switching tools. Move from the headline numbers down to the underlying statements, check the story against the latest news, and frame a valuation — all in the same view. The result is research that holds onto its context, so your thinking carries forward instead of starting over.

Company summary Financials Valuation & DCF News context

Portfolio intelligence

Portfolio views turn raw positions into a clear picture of what is actually happening. Holdings, allocation, performance over time, transaction history, and a live position heatmap are combined so you can see what changed, why it matters, and where your attention should go next. Track contribution and drawdown, spot concentration before it becomes a problem, and review every transaction in context. The focus is deliberately practical: less admin, and a far sharper sense of how the book is really behaving.

Holdings Performance Transactions Position heatmap

Broker support & live trading

Connect the brokers you already use — Interactive Brokers, Zerodha, Upstox, Alpaca, and more — and keep account and execution context right next to your research. Balances, positions, and order workflows stay inside the terminal, so you never have to break focus to act on an idea. Live trading support shrinks the gap between a decision and an order, while account context keeps exposure and risk visible at all times. Research, monitoring, and execution finally share the same screen instead of living in separate apps.

Supported brokers Account context Live trading Execution support

Global & macro context

Markets never move in isolation, and neither should your view of them. Country economic profiles, GDP and trade series, geo-intelligence maps, and relationship graphs help you understand the larger environment behind any single move. Compare economies, follow supply-chain and ownership links, and see how regions and sectors connect on an interactive map. It is this wider context that makes individual decisions sharper — the difference between reacting to a number and understanding what is actually driving it.

Country profiles GDP & trade Geo intelligence Supply links

Screeners, heatmaps & strategy

This is where broad market structure becomes a focused action list. Screeners filter the entire market down to the names that match your criteria, heatmaps reveal sector rotation and momentum at a glance, and the quant studio lets you build, backtest, and refine strategy logic. Move from a high-level scan to specific, tested ideas without ever leaving the environment. Filtering, pattern recognition, and strategy work sit side by side, so research flows naturally into a concrete plan you can act on.
