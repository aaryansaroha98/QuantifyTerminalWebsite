FAQ

Billing questions, answered.

Everything about current Free access, planned paid tiers, and early interest.

Which plan is available right now?

Only the Free plan is available right now. It includes the desktop terminal, core research tools, limited dashboards, AI research queries, watchlists, headlines, sample exports, and delayed market data compared with the starting paid plan.

When are paid plans opening?

Standard, Professional, and Enterprise access are coming soon. Register early interest and we will share launch timing, availability, and discount details first.

Can I register interest before launch?

Yes. Use any Register interest button on this page to email contact@quantifyterminal.com and tell us which plan you want. Early registrants may qualify for launch discounts.

What's the difference between Standard and Professional?

Standard is planned at $199 per month for real-time live data, dashboards, charts, watchlists, screeners, portfolio risk views, alerts, and single-symbol strategy review. Professional is planned at $349 per month for users who need low-latency premium feeds, full Quant Studio, Python and Strategy DSL authoring, the full 35+ workspace / 450+ panel catalog, advanced panel controls, portfolio-aware multi-asset backtesting, advanced quant analytics, Monte Carlo risk lab, options/derivatives strategy tools, real broker connectivity, order routing and execution monitoring, algo publishing, unlimited exports, and priority support.

How does Enterprise pricing work?

Enterprise is custom. It is designed for teams of 10+ that need SSO, shared workspaces, audit logs, SLA coverage, dedicated support, onboarding, and custom integrations.

Do you offer student or academic pricing?

The Free plan is built for students, academic researchers, and evaluators. For classroom or research-team programs, reach out and we'll help you get set up.
