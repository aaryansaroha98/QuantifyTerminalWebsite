FAQ

Billing questions, answered.

Everything about trials, upgrades, and Enterprise contracts.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Every paid plan starts with a full 7-day free trial of Standard, with no credit card required. You get real-time data and unlimited workspaces for the trial period.

What happens when the trial ends?

If you don't choose a paid plan, your account simply reverts to the Free tier with 15-minute delayed data. Nothing is charged and you keep access to the platform.

Can I switch plans or cancel anytime?

Yes. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel at any time from your billing settings. Plan changes are prorated, and annual plans can be paid monthly or up front.

What's the difference between Standard and Professional?

Standard gives you real-time data, unlimited workspaces, and single-asset backtesting. Professional adds ultra-low-latency feeds, multi-asset backtesting, custom Python strategies, full option Greeks, unlimited exports, and priority support.

How does Enterprise pricing work?

Enterprise is a custom quote with a 3-seat minimum, typically around $99–150 per user per month with volume discounts. It includes SSO, shared workspaces, audit logs, a 99.9% uptime SLA, and a dedicated Customer Success Manager.

Do you offer student or academic pricing?

The Free plan is built for students, academic researchers, and evaluators. For classroom or research-team programs, reach out and we'll help you get set up.
