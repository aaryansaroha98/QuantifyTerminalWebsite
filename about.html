About us

Institutional market infrastructure, built as one system.

Quantify Terminal is a market intelligence and analytics platform for professional capital markets work. We consolidate research, portfolio construction, risk management, quantitative strategy, and algorithmic execution into a single desktop environment engineered for accuracy, speed, and operational reliability.

Professional market work is fragmented across terminals, spreadsheets, data vendors, and execution tools that were never designed to operate as one system.

That fragmentation carries a real cost. Analysts reconcile the same figures across incompatible sources, risk teams work from stale exports, and portfolio managers make decisions on evidence assembled by hand under time pressure. The failure is architectural, not analytical.

Quantify Terminal is built to remove that layer of friction. Market data, fundamentals, valuation, screening, portfolio analytics, risk metrics, backtesting, macro and geopolitical context, and execution tooling operate on shared state inside one application. A position, a thesis, and its risk exposure stay connected end to end.

The platform is engineered as production financial infrastructure: streaming market data, low-latency analytics, reproducible backtests, and a desktop architecture that stays responsive under institutional workloads across multiple monitors.

We are building the analytical layer that professional desks depend on, with the rigour that capital allocation decisions require.
