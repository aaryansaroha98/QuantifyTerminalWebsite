Founder

Aaryan Saroha

Young entrepreneur, builder, and finance enthusiast from India, currently pursuing Electrical Engineering at Indian Institute of Technology Jammu.

X Profile LinkedIn

From an early stage, Aaryan developed a deep interest in financial markets, quantitative finance, technology, and business.

His passion for finance was never limited to simply watching markets. He became deeply interested in understanding how global financial systems work, how quantitative analysis drives decision-making, and how technology can transform the future of finance.

Driven by this passion, he founded Quantify Terminal with the vision of building a powerful next-generation financial platform designed for serious investors, analysts, and market participants.

What makes his journey unique is the combination of engineering and finance. While studying Electrical Engineering, he simultaneously focused on developing advanced financial systems, real-time analytics infrastructure, and data-driven market intelligence tools.

He believes that the future of finance belongs to technology-driven platforms that are fast, intelligent, and data-focused. This belief became the foundation behind Quantify Terminal.

Beyond academics and business, Aaryan enjoys building things from scratch, learning continuously, and turning ambitious ideas into real products. His goal is to create technology that helps people understand markets better and make smarter financial decisions.

For Aaryan, Quantify Terminal is not just a project. It is a long-term vision built from passion, curiosity, and a deep interest in the world of finance.
