System Requirements

Built for modern desktop workstations.

Quantify Terminal runs best on a current laptop or desktop with enough memory, storage, and network access for live market workflows.

macOS

  • Operating systemmacOS 13 Ventura or newer
  • ProcessorApple Silicon or Intel 64-bit
  • Memory8 GB RAM minimum, 16 GB recommended
  • Storage2 GB free space for app and local data

Windows

  • Operating systemWindows 10 or Windows 11
  • Processor64-bit Intel or AMD PC
  • Memory8 GB RAM minimum, 16 GB recommended
  • Storage2 GB free space for app and local data

Linux

  • Operating systemUbuntu 22.04+, Debian 12+, or Fedora 39+
  • Processorx86_64 / amd64 desktop
  • Runtimeglibc 2.35+ with AppImage or DEB support
  • Memory8 GB RAM minimum, 16 GB recommended
  • Storage2 GB free space for app and local data

Connectivity & workspace

  • NetworkInternet access for sign-in, live data, sync, AI, news, maps, and updates
  • Display1440px-wide desktop display recommended for dense market layouts
  • GraphicsModern integrated or dedicated GPU recommended for chart-heavy workflows
