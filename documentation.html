Platform overview

A terminal made of connected workspaces.

Quantify Terminal is organized as a set of professional rooms: market cockpit, research desk, portfolio command center, strategy lab, intelligence map, and operating console.

Core idea Context continuity Primary format Desktop workstation User surface Panels, tables, charts, maps, notes
01

Observe

The dashboard and market data views bring broad market movement, global regions, movers, sectors, currencies, commodities, rates, volatility, crypto, calendars, and live headlines into one observation layer.

02

Investigate

Analysis and equity research views give users a structured path from a symbol to fundamentals, valuation, financial charts, ownership, technicals, filings, products, news, and peers.

03

Organize

Watchlists, screeners, alerts, notes, journals, workbooks, exports, and account controls keep the research trail usable after the first idea appears.

04

Test

Portfolio analytics and strategy tools help users review allocation, risk, correlation, performance, stress, signals, backtests, positions, order context, and trade review.

Workspace model

Panels give the terminal its professional feel.

Professional market work rarely happens in a single table. A screener result, price chart, company profile, portfolio exposure, news headline, and note often need to sit beside each other.

Charts

Use chart context beside data tables, research, portfolio exposure, and live market panels.

Tables

Sort, inspect, compare, and export the structured views that drive daily research work.

Maps

Move from symbol-level research into relationships, supply-chain context, geography, and geopolitics.

Notes

Keep journals, trade logs, research notes, formula references, and review notes close to market context.

Alerts

Set and review monitoring rules so market movement can return to the user at the right time.
