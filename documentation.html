User Guide

Quantify Terminal
documentation

Quantify Terminal is a professional market intelligence desktop that brings live markets, deep company research, charting, global data, trading tools and automation together in one place. This guide walks through every tab — what it is for, what you can do, and how it fits into a daily workflow.

Getting started

Install, sign in, and find your way around

Quantify Terminal is a native desktop app for macOS, Windows and Linux. Get it two official ways so you always have a genuine, up-to-date copy.

  • Official website — download the latest build for your operating system from the downloads page.
  • Microsoft Store — install and auto-update on Windows.

When you first open the terminal, create your account or sign in. You arrive at the Dashboard — your home base. Your watchlists, notes and preferences are saved to your account, so they are waiting for you each time you return.

New here? Start with the Dashboard, add a few names to a Watchlist, then open any symbol in Equity Research to see how much depth is available.

Navigating the app

The layout is designed to stay out of your way. A row of tabs runs across the top — each tab is a different tool, and many have a function-key shortcut. The active tab fills the screen, and a search box lets you jump to any symbol or tool in seconds.

  1. Click a tab at the top, or press its F-key, to switch tools instantly.
  2. Type a ticker or company name into the search box to pull it up from anywhere.
  3. Open any item — a stock, fund or country — to drill into its detailed view.
  4. Use the Workspace and Watchlists to keep the things you care about close at hand.
  5. Switch between light and dark themes to suit your eyes and your room.
Advanced Terminal Controls

Advanced terminal controls across every tab

Beyond the individual tools, a set of workstation-grade features runs through the whole terminal — so you can lay out your workspace, spread it across monitors, and take your data wherever you need it.

Detach any panel or tab

Pop out any panel or tab into its own floating window and place it wherever you like — build the exact layout your workflow needs.

Multi-monitor workspaces

Spread detached tabs and panels across several monitors and keep charts, research and news all visible at once.

Export panel data

Extract the data behind almost any panel to PDF, CSV or other formats to share, archive or take into your own tools.

Built-in spreadsheet

Use the Excel-style spreadsheet built into the app to work with live market data right beside your research.

Excel scripting & API

Drive the built-in spreadsheet with scripts, or connect your own external Excel to the Quantify backend to pull live values into your models.

Live data everywhere

Prices and metrics stream live through the trading day, so detached windows, exports and sheets all stay current.

Tip: detach the panels you watch most, move them to a second monitor, and keep your main screen free for research and charting.

Markets & monitoring

Markets & monitoring

Keep the whole market in view — indices, movers, headlines and events — while you work.

01

Dashboard

Your command center. The Dashboard is the first thing you see, giving you a single-screen read on the whole market the moment you open the terminal.

  • Track major indices, commodities, currencies and crypto in one live snapshot.
  • See how each sector is performing and which stocks are the day's biggest movers.
  • Read the latest market headlines without leaving the screen.
  • Check the economic calendar for events coming up that could move markets.
  • Click any item to open it instantly in a more detailed view.

Dashboard — live market monitor, index snapshot, commodities and forex, sector performance, top movers, news headlines, and economic calendar.

04

Market Data

A complete view of global markets. Use Market Data as your daily pulse-check on where the world's markets are heading.

  • Browse world, Asian, US and European indices side by side.
  • Follow futures, commodities, foreign exchange and crypto in one place.
  • Compare how sectors are performing across different regions.
  • Get an instant sense of the overall market mood.

Market Data — world, Asian, US and European indices alongside futures, commodities, FX, crypto, and sector performance.

03

Watchlist

Follow the symbols you care about. Build as many watchlists as you like and keep your favourite names one glance away.

  • Create separate watchlists for stocks, crypto and anything else you follow.
  • See live prices, daily change and a mini price preview for every symbol.
  • Spot momentum quickly with the built-in movers board.
  • Click any symbol to jump straight into its full detail view.

Watchlist — watchlist manager, momentum board, inline price-chart preview, crypto watchlist, and per-symbol detail.

06

Heatmap

F6

See the entire market in one colourful grid. The Heatmap makes it effortless to spot where money is moving.

  • Instantly identify winners and losers by colour and tile size.
  • Filter by market, by sector or by your own watchlist.
  • Hover over any tile for quick detail.
  • Read the mood of the whole market in a single view.

Heatmap — a performance grid colored by percentage change with a market summary and a movers panel.

12

News

F12

Stay on top of the headlines. The News tab gathers the market's stories into one easy feed.

  • Open any article in a clean, built-in reader.

News — an aggregated news feed with an article viewer and peer-news comparison.

Charting & trading

Charting & trading

Study price action, track what you own, place orders, and automate strategies.

Charts

F4

Professional, multi-panel charting. Study price action across several instruments at once, each on its own chart.

  • Open multiple charts together, each with its own symbol and timeframe.
  • Switch between line, candlestick and other chart styles.
  • Add technical indicators to study trends and momentum.

Portfolio

Keep track of what you own and how it is doing. The Portfolio tab brings your holdings together and shows your performance at a glance.

Portfolio — holdings table, performance chart, transaction history, and per-position detail.

Trade Desk — a live trading chart with trade markers, account summary, an order ticket, and open positions.

Reference

Tips & shortcuts

A few habits make the terminal noticeably faster to work in day to day.

  • Press a function key to jump straight to a tab — no clicking required.
  • Type a ticker into the search box from anywhere to load it fast.
  • Build separate watchlists for the different themes or sectors you follow.
  • Set alerts so the terminal keeps watch and tells you when something moves.
  • Use the Workspace to keep your research, notes and numbers in one place.
  • Try Equity Research and Compare when you want the deepest view of a company.

Frequently asked questions

Which platforms does it run on?

macOS, Windows and Linux — available from the official website, with a Microsoft Store option on Windows.

Do I have to trade to use it?

No. Most of the terminal is built for research and monitoring; trading is optional.

Are my watchlists and notes saved?

Yes. Your watchlists, notes and preferences are kept with your account.

How often do prices update?

Prices stream live through the trading day wherever live data is available.

Can I change how it looks?

Yes. Switch between light and dark themes and set your display preferences in the Account tab.
