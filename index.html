Built around real workflows

Not a landing page idea. A terminal-shaped product.

Quantify Terminal is organized around how market work actually happens: discovery, research, comparison, monitoring, risk, execution preparation, and post-trade review. The interface gives power users a dense workspace without making the product feel chaotic.

Every major area is designed as a working surface. Tables can be exported, panels can be rearranged, charts can sit beside detail views, and symbol context can move through the platform.
