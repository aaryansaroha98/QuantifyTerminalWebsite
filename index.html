Research module

Data that speaks for itself.

Quantify Terminal is shaped around the way market work unfolds: discover a signal, inspect the asset, compare context, pressure-test the idea, and keep the decision trail organized.

  • Market snapshots, movers, sectors, calendars, rates, volatility, crypto, commodities, FX, and live headlines.
  • Company research with financials, valuation, ratios, DCF work, ownership, analyst context, technicals, filings, products, news, and peers.
  • Portfolio views for holdings, allocation, P&L, performance, risk, correlation, dividends, tax lots, reviews, and stress tests.
  • Strategy workflows for rules, backtests, equity curves, signal logs, positions, order prep, and execution review.
