Built for serious workflow

Everything stays close to the decision.

Quantify Terminal brings market intelligence, research, portfolio analytics, news, alerts, and global context into a single workspace. Designed for investors, analysts, and researchers, it keeps every critical signal within reach—so you can spend less time switching tools and more time making informed decisions.

  • 1) Research with conviction Analyze companies, industries, and markets through integrated fundamentals, valuation metrics, filings, earnings data, news, and peer comparisons—all within a continuous research workflow.
  • 2) Understand the bigger picture Track global markets through macroeconomic indicators, commodities, currencies, geopolitical developments, heatmaps, and live market activity to maintain context behind every decision.
  • 3) Turn insight into action Monitor portfolios, manage watchlists, create alerts, evaluate opportunities, and move seamlessly from research to execution with structured, data-driven workflows.
