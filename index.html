How it works

Information to decision.

Five steps, in this order. Every hop stores provenance, point-in-time state, permissions, model version and an audit event.

Evidence

The filing, the transcript, the release — parsed into facts, each with the sentence it came from.

Model

Proposed cell changes, previous value beside new. You approve them.

Thesis

The assumptions that depend on those cells move — stronger, weaker, or broken.

Portfolio

Direct and indirect exposure to the affected drivers is recomputed.

Decision

What you decided, on what evidence, at what model version — replayable.

The thesis object

Your thesis, as a live object

Not a memo in a folder. Assumptions with thresholds — gross margin stays above 42%, hyperscaler capex grows above 20% — each linked to the evidence supporting it, the model cells driving it, and the size of the position depending on it. Versioned, so you can see exactly what you believed in March.

Provenance

Every material figure traces to a source or a deterministic calculation. Reported facts, your assumptions, consensus and our estimates stay visibly separate. Nothing is silently overwritten — material changes arrive as a diff you approve.

Foundation

Built on the Data Fabric.

Every market observation, corporate fact, filing claim, event, relationship and private document carries a canonical entity, a source, a licence profile, point-in-time timestamps and a confidence state. The desk never depends on which vendor supplied a field. Every source carries a licence profile, and entitlements are enforced at retrieval. How we handle data rights →

Point-in-time observation 
effective_at    period or instant the fact describes
published_at    when the source published it
available_at    earliest time Quantify could have known it
ingested_at     when Quantify received it
valid_from / valid_to   version interval for restatements
source_version  immutable vendor / document fingerprint

Entity resolution

Tickers, ISINs, LEIs, CIKs, exchange symbols, aliases and company-name history resolve to one canonical ID.

No look-ahead

Historical research and backtests read available_at, never the filing-period date, so restated numbers cannot leak into the past.

Rights enforcement

Display, non-display, derived, cache, redistribution and AI-retrieval rights are machine-readable per source and enforced at the gateway.
