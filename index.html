How it works

Information to decision.

Terminals tell you what happened. Quantify tells you what changed because it happened. Six source families stream into the Data Fabric, are normalised once, fan out to the intelligence engines, and converge through models, theses, risk and the decision gate to the desk. Every hop stores provenance, point-in-time state, permissions, model version and an audit event.

Platform

Beyond the desk.

The same Data Fabric, entitlements and audit trail extend past the terminal: into a firm's private documents, into its own code and agents, back through market history, and, for institutions, into infrastructure inside their own network.

Private Brain Your documents, permission-aware

Google Drive, SharePoint, Slack, Teams, email, meeting transcripts, Notion and internal databases connect with folder, channel and mailbox permissions inherited. Embedding access is data access: vector indexes carry the same ACLs as the documents they index, so nothing surfaces through an AI answer that a user could not open directly.

Agent Runtime API, Python and MCP

Every capability is callable: company data, field queries, screens, model compilation, thesis health, portfolio risk, deep research, backtests, workflows and decision proposals. The same entitlement, audit and permission checks apply whether the caller is the desktop, Excel, a notebook or an autonomous agent.

Market Recorder Rewind market state

A black-box recorder of prices, estimates, news, events, options, macro releases and model state. Reconstruct everything Quantify knew about a company at 09:31 on any date, run event studies on comparable moves, replay a decision as it was known, or benchmark research agents without future information.

Financial Git Research CI/CD

Models, theses, assumptions and reports are commits. A new or amended filing triggers dependency checks, financial unit tests, a proposed model diff, valuation and portfolio recomputation and research QA, then marks every downstream report stale or refreshed. A reviewer presses Reproduce and gets the same analysis from the same snapshot.

Data Guardian Self-healing market data

Schema drift, stale quotes, missing sequence numbers, feed lag, split mismatches and ticker-mapping errors are detected at the gateway and failed over to an entitled alternate provider without violating data rights. An incident opens with the sample payload, contract tests run, and recovery is verified.

Private deployment Inside your network

For banks, funds and sensitive environments: private retrieval, document store, policy and entitlement engine, immutable audit export and optional local inference run as a containerised deployment inside the client network, receiving licensed public-market and model updates from Quantify Cloud.

Foundation

Built on the Data Fabric.

Every market observation, corporate fact, filing claim, event, relationship and private document carries a canonical entity, a source, a licence profile, point-in-time timestamps and a confidence state. The desk never depends on which vendor supplied a field.

Point-in-time observation 
effective_at    period or instant the fact describes
published_at    when the source published it
available_at    earliest time Quantify could have known it
ingested_at     when Quantify received it
valid_from / valid_to   version interval for restatements
source_version  immutable vendor / document fingerprint

Entity resolution

Tickers, ISINs, LEIs, CIKs, exchange symbols, aliases and company-name history resolve to one canonical ID.

No look-ahead

Historical research and backtests read available_at, never the filing-period date, so restated numbers cannot leak into the past.

Rights enforcement

Display, non-display, derived, cache, redistribution and AI-retrieval rights are machine-readable per source and enforced at the gateway.

World Model

Companies, securities, people, facilities, suppliers, customers, funds, countries, regulators and commodities connect as temporal, confidence-weighted edges.
