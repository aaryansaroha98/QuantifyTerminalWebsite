How it works

Information to decision.

One pipeline, six stages. Every transition stores provenance, point-in-time state, permissions, model version and an audit event. Nothing propagates silently, and nothing propagates without a source.

  1. New information. Filings, earnings releases, transcripts, news, macro prints, corporate actions and private documents enter with canonical identifiers, point-in-time timestamps and data rights attached.
  2. Knowledge Compiler. Documents become facts, claims, entities, relationships and evidence. Every claim links to the exact span that supports it.
  3. Causal and expectation engines. The change is traced through suppliers, customers, geographies and factors, tested against consensus and what price already embeds, and weighed against competing explanations.
  4. Model and thesis. The Financial Model Compiler proposes cell-level updates with source lineage. The thesis graph re-scores each assumption and reports health with its components exposed.
  5. Portfolio Twin. First-, second- and third-order exposure is recomputed across holdings, hedges, currencies and hidden dependencies. Failure paths and liquidity are stressed, not assumed.
  6. Decision. Expected return distribution, thesis health, portfolio fit and mandate compliance are assembled into a decision that a human approves, with an immutable record of why.

Deterministic by construction. Language models plan, interpret and explain. Financial mathematics runs in deterministic, versioned code. Scores expose their components, backtests read only what was knowable, and nothing reaches a portfolio without a human gate.

Foundation

Built on the Data Fabric.

Every market observation, corporate fact, filing claim, event, relationship and private document carries a canonical entity, a source, a licence profile, point-in-time timestamps and a confidence state. The desk never depends on which vendor supplied a field.

Point-in-time observation 
effective_at    period or instant the fact describes
published_at    when the source published it
available_at    earliest time Quantify could have known it
ingested_at     when Quantify received it
valid_from / valid_to   version interval for restatements
source_version  immutable vendor / document fingerprint

Entity resolution

Tickers, ISINs, LEIs, CIKs, exchange symbols, aliases and company-name history resolve to one canonical ID.

No look-ahead

Historical research and backtests read available_at, never the filing-period date, so restated numbers cannot leak into the past.

Rights enforcement

Display, non-display, derived, cache, redistribution and AI-retrieval rights are machine-readable per source and enforced at the gateway.

World Model

Companies, securities, people, facilities, suppliers, customers, funds, countries, regulators and commodities connect as temporal, confidence-weighted edges.
