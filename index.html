How it works

Information to decision.

One pipeline, six stages. Every transition stores provenance, point-in-time state, permissions, model version and an audit event. Nothing propagates silently, and nothing propagates without a source.

  1. New information. Filings, earnings releases, transcripts, news, macro prints, corporate actions and private documents enter with canonical identifiers, point-in-time timestamps and data rights attached.
  2. Knowledge Compiler. Documents become facts, claims, entities, relationships and evidence. Every claim links to the exact span that supports it.
  3. Causal and expectation engines. The change is traced through suppliers, customers, geographies and factors, tested against consensus and what price already embeds, and weighed against competing explanations.
  4. Model and thesis. The Financial Model Compiler proposes cell-level updates with source lineage. The thesis graph re-scores each assumption and reports health with its components exposed.
  5. Portfolio Twin. First-, second- and third-order exposure is recomputed across holdings, hedges, currencies and hidden dependencies. Failure paths and liquidity are stressed, not assumed.
  6. Decision. Expected return distribution, thesis health, portfolio fit and mandate compliance are assembled into a decision that a human approves, with an immutable record of why.

Deterministic by construction. Language models plan, interpret and explain. Financial mathematics runs in deterministic, versioned code. Scores expose their components, backtests read only what was knowable, and nothing reaches a portfolio without a human gate.

Systems

Systems, not tabs.

Each system shares the same event bus, evidence graph, model registry, thesis graph and Portfolio Twin. Together they maintain your financial state. The desk is the surface; this is what runs underneath it.

Pulse

The always-on anomaly and relevance engine. Pulse ranks what changed across price, fundamentals, narrative, positioning, macro and your own network, personalised to your portfolio, watchlists and thesis assumptions, and answers why it matters now.

Deep Research

A multi-step evidence engine, not a longer chat answer. An orchestrator plans, calls deterministic financial tools and retrieval, builds an evidence graph, and a verifier tests every claim against sources and conflicts before a report is written and saved to memory.

Financial Model Compiler

Filings, earnings releases and guidance compile directly into versioned financial models. Tables, XBRL and management commentary become proposed cell updates with source lineage. Recalculation is deterministic. The analyst approves the diff and commits a new model version.

Thesis Health

Scattered notes, PDFs and models become a machine-readable thesis. Each assumption depends on observable metrics, events or claims, carries invalidation conditions, and reports health as a transparent composite of status, evidence freshness, contradiction strength and model sensitivity.

Portfolio Digital Twin

A living representation of the whole portfolio: holdings, derivatives, factors, currencies, suppliers, customers, geographies, theses and liquidity. Failure search finds the shock combinations that produce a −10%, −20% or −30% outcome and names the assumptions responsible.

Decision Ledger

Reasoning recorded as rigorously as a broker records transactions. Every decision stores intent, sizing, conditions, kill conditions, evidence IDs, model version, constitution check and approver, so it can be replayed exactly as it was known at the time.

Workflow Engine

A natural-language operating layer for the entire terminal. Give it an objective. It produces an explicit plan, invokes terminal functions, shows the execution trace, and asks for confirmation only at high-impact boundaries such as portfolio changes and live orders.

Investor Chat

A professional network where every conversation carries live financial objects. Rooms attach to tickers, themes, private deals and real estate. Ticket-size gated data rooms, research battles and timestamped track records turn discussion into proprietary, permissioned intelligence.

What the fabric guarantees
  • Entity resolutionTickers, ISINs, LEIs, CIKs, exchange symbols, aliases and company-name history resolve to one canonical ID.
  • No look-aheadHistorical research and backtests read available_at, never the filing-period date, so restated numbers cannot leak into the past.
  • Rights enforcementDisplay, non-display, derived, cache, redistribution and AI-retrieval rights are machine-readable per source and enforced at the gateway.
  • World ModelCompanies, securities, people, facilities, suppliers, customers, funds, countries, regulators and commodities connect as temporal, confidence-weighted edges.
  • Quality pipelineSchema validation, identifier resolution, unit and currency normalisation, corporate-action adjustment, duplicate detection, cross-source reconciliation, anomaly checks, versioning, provenance.
